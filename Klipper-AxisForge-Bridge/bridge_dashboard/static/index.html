    Preflight required

Klipper sets the motion timeline. The robot must accept each timed chunk before Klipper queues its extrusion. Both systems stop at chunk boundaries and wait for completion.

Upload G-code

Upload the original file to Klipper and stage it for AxisForge. Uploading does not heat or move either system.

Job preflight

No job selected

Choose which heaters to check. Preflight heats only the selected heaters to their G-code targets, then switches all heaters off before checking the robot. Robot preflight does not move the arm.

Preflight heater checks

Configured heaters still follow G-code at Start. If a heater is not configured and its check is off, its commands are ignored for this job. Off targets (0 °C) need no heat test.

    Start reheats configured heaters and moves the arm. Keep the work area clear and the UR speed slider at 100%. Run preflight again after changing heater options. Results expire after 15 minutes or when the file or calibration changes.
